How estimates work.

All amounts are annual except monthly costs, hourly figures and percentages. Net = gross plus bonuses less business expenses, entered social insurance, income tax and personal pension cash contributions. Pension tax relief is not modeled; employer pension is shown separately and is not spendable income. Gross-to-net assumes salary excludes employer pension. Net-to-gross uses a numerical search with inputs held constant. Equal installments are illustrative, not payroll schedules.

Manual effective rates apply to gross less entered business expenses and social insurance; verify that this base matches your local regime. Australia uses resident marginal rates for 2026–27 and a user-adjustable Medicare assumption, without tax offsets. Swiss canton/municipality, Spanish autonomous community, Italian regional and municipal taxes and special freelancer schemes require separate verification. Benchmark data is not a role-specific salary prediction.

Calculations run in your browser. Only public exchange-rate data is fetched; no salary inputs are transmitted.
